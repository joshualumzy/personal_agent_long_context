# Hiring proposals: backtest

Generated by `scripts/backtest-hiring.ts` over the 60 working days 2026-01-01 to 2026-03-25. The rules (`src/hiring-proposals.ts`) read only domain health (`src/domain-health.ts`). They are scored here against OrgForge's own record of departures, hires and hand-overs (`eval/orgforge/gap-truth.json`), which they never see.

## Rules and thresholds

- **orphaned**: the owner has left and nobody has taken the domain on, for 2+ working days running. With 1 day, legacy auth service, mobile analytics and Project Titan are proposed on 2026-01-01 only because their hand-overs are recorded on the 2nd.
- **thin**: over a full 30-day window, at most 25% of the median domain's contributors. The split in this corpus is wide: on 2026-01-30 kubernetes-deploy and terraform-infra have 5 each, the next domain 16, and the median 21. Before a full window, everything looks thin (on 2026-01-01 eight domains have ≤ 2).
- **overloaded**: the owner holds 3+ domains and this one had 2+ incidents in 30 days. With 1 incident it fires on TitanDB from 2026-01-19, when Janice took on three domains and ENG-137 arrived. With 2, it fires from 2026-01-29.

## Summary

- 3 of the 3 hires inside the record were preceded by a proposal for a domain they brought, by 7, 6, 6 days.
- 6 proposals in 60 working days. 3 were followed by a hire, 2 by someone inside taking the domain on, and 1 by neither.
- Caveats: three hires is a very small sample. The thin threshold was chosen after looking at this same corpus, so this is not an out-of-sample result. The record has no hires after 2026-02-05, so later proposals cannot be confirmed either way. Reese and Ethan Patel were hired before Morgan left (a planned layoff), so the departure rule alone could not have preceded them; the thin rule did.
