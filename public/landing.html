Made by a small team that makes wearables.

Stellar Ark builds AI-native wearables: context glasses, a pendant and this ring. Kaki is the agent that runs on their company records at work. Calls reach it through a browser tab; meetings at a table reach it through the ring on your finger.

At a small company, the answer already exists. Finding it is the job.

It sits in Slack, Jira, Confluence, email and one colleague's memory. People dig it out by hand before every call, write the follow-ups from memory after, and notice they need to hire only when the same three people get paged for every incident.

One Tuesday with Kaki

You are a backend engineer at a small company. At 11, you meet a customer your team just let down. Here is how the day goes.

  1. 09:00

    Answers, with receipts

    You ask if last week's outage is the same bug as February's. Kaki reads the tickets, postmortems and chats: not the same bug, but the same piece of code. Every line opens the record it came from.

  2. 09:05

    See how it connects

    The answer comes with a map of what it rests on: both incidents, the fixes, the people who wrote them up. Pick a past date and Kaki answers from only what was known that day.

  3. 11:00

    On the call, it keeps track

    Kaki writes the call down line by line, from the call's tab or from the ring on your finger. When someone says plan A after the team agreed on B, it flags the conflict.

  4. 12:00

    Promises become drafts

    When the call ends, each promise is a draft email, ticket or meeting, and each shows whose words it came from.

  5. 15:00

    It sees the gap early

    Kaki tracks who knows what. When part of the system is left with too few people, it proposes a hire.

  6. 16:00

    From “we need someone” to outreach

    You confirm what the role needs. Kaki searches public profiles, puts the best matches in the middle and says why each one fits. It drafts the outreach.

Kaki suggests. You decide.

Nothing sends itself

Approving opens your own Gmail, calendar or tracker with the draft filled in. The last click is always yours.

Edit it, approve it again

Approval belongs to the exact draft you read. Change a word and it asks again.

Money goes to its owner

A discount promised on a call goes to Finance, not to you and not to Kaki.

Secrets never reach the model

Keys, personal IDs and planted instructions in a transcript are held back before any model reads them.
