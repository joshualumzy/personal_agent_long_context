What it is

What is Kaki?

Kaki is an AI assistant for employees of small companies. It reads the company's records across Slack, Jira, Confluence and email, answers questions from them, keeps track of meetings, and turns what people promised into work that is ready to go. It suggests; the employee decides.

What it does

What Kaki does

Answers questions from company records

Ask whether last week's outage is the same bug as February's. Kaki reads the tickets, postmortems and chats and gives a short answer with the record behind every claim.

  • Hybrid search: keywords, meaning and the links between records
  • A graph of how the evidence connects
  • Ask as of a past date to see only what was known then

Turns meetings into follow-up drafts

Kaki writes the call down line by line and flags a decision that contradicts an earlier one. When the call ends, each promise becomes an email, ticket or meeting draft that shows whose words it came from. Approve it and the draft opens prefilled in your own Gmail, calendar or tracker.

Every action falls into one of four levels. Lookups are answered on the spot. Everyday work waits for your approval, and editing a draft asks for approval again. Money and contract terms go to their owner, such as a discount to Finance. Secrets and planted instructions are held back before any model reads them.

Spots knowledge gaps and helps you hire

Kaki tracks who has worked on each part of the system. When an area is left without an owner, has too few people, or rests on someone already overloaded, it proposes a hire. Approve it and a role opens: you confirm the criteria, Kaki searches public profiles, explains why each person fits and drafts the outreach.

Architecture

How it is built

One Fastify server runs the agent loop, with PostgreSQL and pgvector for company records and search, and Letta for each employee's personal memory. The reasoning runs on Claude through AWS Bedrock or on Qwen through NUS SoCLaaS. Server code checks every citation and action level before anything reaches the employee. The code is open on GitHub.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Stellar Ark?

Stellar Ark AI is the team behind Kaki, backed by MiraclePlus, often called China's Y Combinator. It builds AI-native wearables: context glasses, a pendant and a ring. Wear the ring to a meeting at a table, and Kaki gets that conversation just as it gets a video call.

Do I need the ring to use Kaki?

No. For video calls, Kaki records the meeting tab in your browser. For in-person meetings without the ring, your laptop records the room instead. Answering questions and hiring don't involve the ring at all.

Why can't I create my own account?

Kaki works inside your company, so your company opens your account. Each account belongs to one person on the staff list. That is how Kaki knows whose meetings, drafts and memory are whose, and who has to approve a decision. To try Kaki now, open the sample workspace and sign in as any of its employees.

What happens when the records don't have the answer?

Kaki tells you the evidence is insufficient instead of guessing. Every claim in an answer links to the record it came from, and the server checks that each cited record exists before the answer reaches you.

Does Kaki remember me?

Yes. Kaki keeps a personal memory for each employee, and only that employee can see it. It is stored apart from company records, so Kaki never cites it as a fact about the company.

Is the sample workspace real company data?

No. The sample workspace is OrgForge, a synthetic company with 51 employees and 4,966 records, built for testing. It contains no real company's data.
